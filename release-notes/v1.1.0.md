## WatchTower v1.1.0

A look-and-feel release: more themes, a gradient theme you design yourself, more fonts and properly rounded dropdown menus. Your data and settings are untouched.

### New
- **Twenty new dark themes**, each tuned as a calm, low-glare palette: Obsidian Calm, Deep Teal, Northern Aurora, Arctic Twilight, Indigo Dusk, Plum Velvet, Amethyst Night, Wine Cellar, Ember Charcoal, Copper Night, Olive Grove, Moss Stone, Cedar Wood, Graphite Blue, Ink Navy, Charcoal Mint, Storm Cloud, Midnight Rose, Dusk Gold and Space Violet.
- **Custom Gradient theme** (*Settings > Appearance > Theme*): choose two or three colours, the gradient direction and your own accent colour, or start from one of eight presets. Text and surfaces adapt automatically, so a light or a dark gradient always stays readable. The result is saved with your settings and exported with your data.
- **Sixteen more fonts**: Times New Roman, Georgia, Cambria, Palatino Linotype, Garamond, Book Antiqua, Calibri, Arial, Verdana, Tahoma, Trebuchet MS and Cascadia Code (Windows system fonts), plus **Merriweather, Source Serif, Playfair Display and IBM Plex Sans**, which are bundled and work offline.

### Improved
- **Rounded dropdown menus** everywhere (fonts, filters, sort order, categories, landing page, backup frequency, heat map range): themed, animated, with a check mark on the current choice and full keyboard control (arrow keys, Enter, Escape, type to jump). They open upward near the bottom of the window and are never clipped by cards. The font list previews every font in its own typeface.

### Fixed
- Settings could show *"This page couldn't load"* after installing over an older copy. A stale page cache left by the previous version is now cleared every time WatchTower starts.

### Notes
- Windows builds are not code-signed, so SmartScreen may warn on first launch (*More info > Run anyway*).
- System fonts such as Times New Roman or Calibri fall back to a similar font on computers that do not have them.
- Verify your download with `SHA256SUMS.txt`.
