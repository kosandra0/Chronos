## Chronos v0.1.2 - Themes, motion and polish

### New
- **Twelve themes**: ten new soothing palettes (Midnight Ocean, Forest Mist, Lavender Dusk, Warm Mocha, Slate Stone, Soft Sunset, Desert Sand, Rose Quartz, Nordic Frost, Fresh Mint) plus Classic Dark/Light and Follow System, with a live preview picker.
- **Ten bundled fonts** that work offline: Inter, Manrope, Nunito, Poppins, DM Sans, Lora, JetBrains Mono, Segoe UI, System UI, Monospace.
- **Rounded corners 0-24 px**, applied to the whole floating app frame, cards, buttons and dialogs.
- **Command palette** (Ctrl+K): jump to any page, switch theme, toggle common settings.
- **Daily screen-time goal** with an animated progress bar on the Overview.
- New settings: animation speed (calm / normal / snappy), shadow depth, number roll-up, reset appearance.

### Motion
- Sliding navigation indicator, staggered rising cards, tactile hover/press feedback, smooth theme cross-fade.
- Numbers roll up smoothly when they appear or change.

### Your data
Existing data under `%APPDATA%\Chronos` is kept on upgrade. Settings from earlier versions continue to work.

### Downloads
- `Chronos-Setup-0.1.2.exe` - installer
- `Chronos-Portable-0.1.2-x64.exe` - portable

Unsigned builds: Windows SmartScreen may warn on first launch.
